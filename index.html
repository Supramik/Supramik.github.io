Main project: digitizing document control

The problem

The quality management system's document control process covers how controlled documents are created, revised, withdrawn, re-approved, and reinstated. It ran on shared drives, spreadsheets, and hand stamps.

What I did
  • Ran requirements gathering with the document control team and mapped the current process. The six documented procedures turned out to be one skeleton with variations: four patterns that share one closing.
  • Designed the To-Be for three process pools in draw.io, at overview and detailed levels, and presented it to my supervisor and the business user.
  • Built and tested it in the development workspace of the company's document management system, IComTeq IMS. The build included an 11-step approval workflow, a copy-request workflow, an 18-tag tag set that replaced the physical stamps, fail-safe approval gates, and a controlled folder structure.
  • Recorded fourteen behaviors of the platform that shaped the design, including a tag-overwrite problem I worked around.
  • Wrote the Functional Specifications Document and handed over the project on my last day.
Result

A published, tested workflow in the development workspace, a specification for the next phase, and a documented list of open items.

Quality assurance

  • Tested a key retail customer's sales conversion end to end, from the sales order to the printed invoice and its posting to the finance system, before the change was deployed to production.
  • Ran Android 16 compatibility testing of the field sales tablet apps over two cycles, with screen recordings as evidence for the developers.

Also
